Frequently asked questions

TikTok Shop is TikTok's in-app commerce platform where products are discovered through the algorithmic For You Page, creator affiliate links, and Live Shopping streams. The product feed is a CSV file uploaded via TikTok Shop Seller Center or hosted at a URL for TikTok to fetch automatically.
TikTok requires the currency code (GBP) as a separate column — not embedded in the price field like Google accepts. Feeds imported from Google-compatible CSV often fail because they include "12.50 GBP" in the price field. Trajekt emits a numeric-only price column and a separate currency column.
60 characters is TikTok's algorithmic preference for FYP cards. TikTok's UI truncates titles aggressively and favours short, punchy names over long descriptive ones. Trajekt applies a 60-char truncation rule for TikTok feeds — different from your Google or Meta title strategy.
9 required fields: sku_id (or id for ads-only), title, description, availability, condition, price, currency (separate column), link, and image_link. TikTok supports up to 27 fields total.
video_link accepts a URL pointing to a TikTok-native short-form video for your product. This field dramatically improves For You Page placement. Trajekt maps Shopify product videos to video_link automatically when available.
Yes. TikTok Shop is available in the UK and is one of the most active markets globally. Register at seller-uk.tiktok.com. UK fulfilment SLA: typically 2 business days.
Catalogue Ads are paid formats using your product data — Video Shopping Ads, Collection Ads, and Dynamic Showcase Ads. TikTok offers 50+ video templates that generate short-form video ads using your catalogue data automatically.
TikTok supports hourly refresh schedules. For fast-moving fashion or electronics, hourly updates prevent showing out-of-stock products. Daily is sufficient for most merchants.
Use sku_id for TikTok Shop (in-app commerce). Use id for advertising-only catalogues not linked to TikTok Shop. Using the wrong identifier causes catalogue sync failures.
