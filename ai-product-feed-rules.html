Features

AI Product Feed Rules

✍️

Title rewriting

AI Rules can rewrite product titles to include brand, material, colour, and size in the optimal order for each category — at scale, across your entire catalogue.

📝

Description enrichment

Thin descriptions kill Shopping performance. AI Rules generate factual, keyword-rich descriptions from your product title, category, and attributes — suitable for both Google Shopping and ChatGPT.

🎯

Attribute extraction

AI can extract colour, material, style, and other attributes from unstructured product titles and descriptions — populating missing feed fields automatically.

🧠

Channel-specific copy

Write different descriptions for different channels. A Google Shopping description is different from a ChatGPT-optimised one. AI Rules can generate both from the same source product.

💰

Cache & credits

AI results are cached per product — if a product doesn't change, the cached result is used and no credits are consumed. Credits are only used for new or changed products.

📊

Rules targeting

Target AI Rules at specific product types, categories, or conditions. Only rewrite descriptions for products with less than 50 words. Only extract colour for apparel. Full control.

Frequently asked questions

AI product feed rules are instructions that apply Claude AI to specific fields in your product feed at scale. For example: "Rewrite the description for all products with fewer than 100 words in the description field, making it factual and keyword-rich for Google Shopping." Trajekt processes your entire catalogue through these rules automatically.
Trajekt uses Claude (by Anthropic) for AI feed rules. Claude is particularly well-suited for product data enrichment because of its ability to follow precise instructions, maintain consistent formatting, and generate factual content without hallucination.
Trajekt uses a credit system for AI Rules — typically fractions of a penny per product. A basic description rewrite costs approximately £0.001 per product. For a 10,000-product catalogue, a full description refresh costs approximately £10. Credits are purchased as needed and results are cached to avoid re-processing unchanged products.
When Trajekt processes a product through an AI Rule, the result is cached against a hash of the product data. If the product hasn't changed in your next feed run, the cached result is used and no credits are consumed. This means ongoing feed costs are very low — only new or updated products consume credits.
The most impactful AI rules for Google Shopping: 1) Title rewriting to include brand + key attributes in optimal order, 2) Description enrichment adding missing specifications and use cases, 3) Attribute extraction for colour, material, size from unstructured text, 4) Category improvement — better google_product_category assignment. These directly impact impression share and CTR.
