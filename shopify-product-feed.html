Common Shopify feed problems — and how Trajekt fixes them

Price mismatches — Sale prices changing in Shopify but not reflected in your feed until the next crawl. Trajekt’s daily refresh (or twice-daily on Growth+) keeps prices current. A manual re-run can be triggered any time before a sale launches.
Missing GTINs — Shopify doesn’t have a native barcode field in all versions. Trajekt maps your custom barcode/EAN attribute directly to the gtin feed field via a rule. Branded products get their GTINs; own-brand products get identifier_exists: no.
Thumbnail images — Some Shopify configurations export thumbnail image URLs rather than full-size images. Trajekt maps to the full-size image URL via the API, ensuring images meet Google’s 800×800px recommended minimum.
Unoptimised titles — Default Shopify product names are written for on-site browsing, not Shopping search matching. Trajekt rules restructure titles to the Shopping-optimal format (Brand + Product + Attributes) without changing anything in your Shopify store.
No custom labels — Shopify has no native custom label concept for Shopping. Trajekt sets custom_label_0–4 automatically via rules — by price range, category, stock level, or any field in your Shopify data.
