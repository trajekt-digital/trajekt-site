FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Kelkoo is a European price comparison shopping engine founded in France and operating in 15 European markets including the UK. It aggregates product listings from retailers and presents them to shoppers comparing prices. Unlike Google Shopping, Kelkoo specialises exclusively in price comparison and is particularly strong in the UK, France, Germany, and Scandinavia.
Kelkoo uses a CPC (cost per click) model — merchants pay when a shopper clicks their listing to visit the product page. You upload your product feed to Kelkoo's merchant centre, set your CPC bids per category, and your products appear in comparison results. Kelkoo also distributes listings to partner sites across its network.
Kelkoo uses an XML feed format. Required fields include EAN (barcode), product name, description, category, brand, price, currency, availability, image URL, and product URL. The field names and structure differ from Google Shopping XML.
Google Shopping uses auction-based CPCs and appears in Google Search results. Kelkoo is a dedicated price comparison site where shoppers go specifically to compare prices. Kelkoo typically reaches more price-sensitive buyers who are further along in the purchase decision. Both charge CPC, but Kelkoo's inventory is typically cheaper than Google Shopping for the same categories.
Yes. EAN (European Article Number) barcodes are important for Kelkoo's product matching — allowing Kelkoo to compare the same product across multiple retailers. Include accurate EANs in your Trajekt feed for Kelkoo to maximise your listing's visibility in comparison results.
Categories with strong price comparison activity on Kelkoo in the UK include: consumer electronics, household appliances, computers and peripherals, photography equipment, and sports equipment. Fashion and accessories also perform well. Luxury and premium brands may see less benefit as price comparison is less relevant for brand-exclusive products.
Register at kelkoo.co.uk/merchants. Kelkoo reviews merchant applications for quality and compliance. Once approved, you upload your product feed and set CPC bids by category. A dedicated Kelkoo account manager typically assists with onboarding.
Yes. Kelkoo operates in 15 European markets: UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, and Portugal. From one Trajekt connection, you can generate country-specific Kelkoo feeds with localised pricing and currency.
Daily updates are strongly recommended. Kelkoo is a price comparison engine — an out-of-date price that differs from your website causes shoppers to bounce immediately, damaging your Kelkoo quality score and costing you CPC spend without converting.
CPC bids vary significantly by category. Electronics: 10-40p per click. Fashion: 15-50p. Sports: 15-40p. Home & garden: 10-30p. Start with Kelkoo's recommended bids for your category and optimise based on conversion rate. Monitor cost per acquisition and adjust bids accordingly.
Kelkoo primarily operates on a paid CPC model. However, Kelkoo occasionally offers introductory free credits for new merchants. Check kelkoo.co.uk for current promotions.
