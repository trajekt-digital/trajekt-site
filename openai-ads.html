How OpenAI Ads work — and why they’re different

ChatGPT Ads don’t work like Google Ads. Understanding the difference is critical to getting your feed right.

Google Shopping Ads

‣Dedicated Shopping tab — separate from search results
‣Keyword-adjacent matching via product feed titles
‣Bid dominates placement — higher bid wins in similar quality
‣Product title is primary matching signal
‣Description is secondary — mostly for matching, not shown
‣GTIN completeness is a strong positive signal

OpenAI / ChatGPT Ads

✓Context-native — appear inside conversational AI responses
✓Semantic matching — LLM understands query intent, not just keywords
✓Relevance score (from description quality) combines with bid
✓Description is the primary signal — the LLM reads and evaluates it
✓Product must genuinely answer the user’s specific query
✓Feed completeness across all fields improves eligibility

The key implication: description quality is your quality score

In Google Ads, quality score is a combination of expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience. In OpenAI Ads, the equivalent signal is how well your product description answers the user’s specific conversational query — evaluated by the same LLM that generated the response.

A vague description ("great jacket, multiple colours") at a high bid will lose to a specific description ("waterproof Pertex Equilibrium nylon shell with 15,000mm HH rating, taped seams, adjustable storm hood, packable to chest pocket — designed for sustained rain in UK upland conditions") at a lower bid. This is fundamentally unlike any other advertising channel and requires a different approach to feed optimisation.

OpenAI Ads — frequently asked questions

OpenAI Ads (also referred to as ChatGPT Ads or ChatGPT Shopping Ads) are sponsored product placements that appear within ChatGPT’s responses to shopping-related queries. When a user asks ChatGPT to recommend products, relevant sponsored listings appear alongside organic results. The ads platform uses the same JSONL product feed as ChatGPT’s organic shopping layer.
As of mid-2026, OpenAI’s shopping features and sponsored placements are rolling out globally with UK access expanding. Merchants registered in the OpenAI merchant programme are positioned to receive early access as UK paid placements become available. Getting registered and indexed now is the critical first step.
The process has three steps: (1) Register your store at chatgpt.com/merchants to join OpenAI’s merchant programme and receive SFTP credentials. (2) Generate a JSONL product feed from your Shopify or WooCommerce store — Trajekt does this automatically. (3) Configure SFTP delivery so your feed is uploaded to OpenAI’s endpoint on a daily schedule. Trajekt handles steps 2 and 3 automatically once you add your OpenAI SFTP credentials.
Google Shopping primarily matches products to queries via title keywords and GTIN completeness. ChatGPT Shopping evaluates product descriptions using the same LLM that processes the user’s query — meaning description quality, specificity, and factual accuracy are the primary relevance signals, not keyword density. A detailed, factual description that genuinely explains what the product is, what it’s made of, what it’s used for, and who it suits will significantly outperform a keyword-stuffed title with a thin description in ChatGPT placements.
