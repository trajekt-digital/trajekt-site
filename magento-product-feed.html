REST API connection

Trajekt connects via Magento's standard REST API. Create an integration in Magento admin with read-only product permissions and paste the token into Trajekt.

📦

Custom attributes

Magento's EAV attribute system is supported. Map any product attribute — including custom ones created for your store — to the correct field in your output feeds.

Multi-store support

Magento multi-store view architecture is supported. Generate separate feeds per store view for different markets, languages, or brands.

Magento tier and special prices are pulled correctly. Trajekt maps special price as sale_price with the correct effective date range.

🔄

Scheduled sync

Trajekt syncs with your Magento store on schedule and regenerates feeds automatically — capturing new products, price changes, and stock updates.

Common Magento feed problems — and how Trajekt fixes them

Price mismatches — Sale prices changing in Magento but not reflected in your feed until the next crawl. Trajekt’s daily refresh (or twice-daily on Growth+) keeps prices current. A manual re-run can be triggered any time before a sale launches.
Missing GTINs — Magento doesn’t have a native barcode field in all versions. Trajekt maps your custom barcode/EAN attribute directly to the gtin feed field via a rule. Branded products get their GTINs; own-brand products get identifier_exists: no.
Thumbnail images — Some Magento configurations export thumbnail image URLs rather than full-size images. Trajekt maps to the full-size image URL via the API, ensuring images meet Google’s 800×800px recommended minimum.
Unoptimised titles — Default Magento product names are written for on-site browsing, not Shopping search matching. Trajekt rules restructure titles to the Shopping-optimal format (Brand + Product + Attributes) without changing anything in your Magento store.
No custom labels — Magento has no native custom label concept for Shopping. Trajekt sets custom_label_0–4 automatically via rules — by price range, category, stock level, or any field in your Magento data.
