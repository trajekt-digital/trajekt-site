Google Shopping Feed Optimisation Troubleshooting

Zero-Impression Products: Why They Happen and What to Do About Them

August 13, 2026  ·  8 min read

Zero-impression products are one of the most frustrating Google Shopping problems: your products are approved in Merchant Center, your campaign is active with budget, and yet some products never appear in any Shopping results. They show zero impressions in Google Ads product group reports, zero in the Google Merchant Center Products tab, and no traffic. They're effectively invisible.

Understanding why this happens requires understanding that Google Shopping is not a simple feed-in, show-out system. Even approved products go through a quality and relevance filtering layer before they appear in Shopping results.

The three reasons products get zero impressions

1. Query relevance mismatch. Google can't find queries that match your product well enough to show it. This is almost always a title or description problem. If your product title is "SKU-12345-NAV-XL" rather than "Barbour Beaufort Jacket Men Navy XL", Google has no signal for which queries this product is relevant to. It's approved and in the feed — but relevance scoring keeps it out of results.

2. Bid too low to compete. Google Shopping auctions are competitive. If your bid is too low to win any auction for the queries your product is eligible for, you get zero impressions. This is more common in high-competition categories and with low-quality products that have a weak relevance score (meaning they need a higher bid to overcome the quality deficit).

3. Excluded by campaign structure. The product is in your feed but excluded from your campaigns by a product group filter, a campaign-level product filter, or because it's in a campaign that's paused or out of budget.

How to diagnose which products have zero impressions

In Google Ads: Products tab → filter by Impressions = 0 over the last 90 days. This gives you the complete list. Cross-reference against your Merchant Center Products tab to confirm they're approved. If approved and in a live campaign but still zero impressions after 7+ days, proceed to diagnosis.

Important caveat

New products often have zero impressions for their first 48–72 hours while Google processes them. Filter for products that have been in your feed and approved for at least 7 days before treating zero impressions as a problem to solve.

The feed audit for zero-impression products

For each zero-impression product, check these in order:

Title quality: Does the title contain the words a buyer would search to find this product? If the title is a product code, a vague description, or the manufacturer's internal name, it's not query-matchable. Rewrite using: Brand + Product type + Key attributes + Colour + Size.

Category assignment: Is the google_product_category set to the most specific applicable category? Products in overly broad categories (just "Apparel & Accessories" rather than "Apparel & Accessories > Clothing > Outerwear > Coats & Jackets") have weaker relevance signals for category-specific queries.

GTIN completeness: Missing GTINs on branded products reduce impression share. Google uses GTINs to match products to established product records — branded products without GTINs compete at a disadvantage against those with them.

Image quality: Small or low-quality images can reduce Shopping placement rates even for approved products. Check that your image URL resolves to an image above 800×800px.

Using Trajekt rules to fix zero-impression products at scale

When you identify the pattern in zero-impression products — they all lack brand in the title, they're all in the wrong category, they all have missing GTINs — you can fix the whole group with a single Trajekt rule rather than editing products one by one in your store.

# Title rule — zero-impression products missing brand IF title NOT STARTS WITH vendor THEN title = vendor + " " + title # Category rule — overcategorised products IF product_type CONTAINS "Waterproof Jacket" THEN google_product_category = 5697 # GTIN rule — branded products missing identifier IF gtin IS EMPTY AND vendor IS NOT EMPTY THEN identifier_exists = "no" # (and flag for manual GTIN lookup)

Rules run on every daily feed refresh. Once you've identified and fixed the root cause for a product type, all current and future products of that type get the fix automatically. This is the leverage point: fixing one rule affects hundreds of products simultaneously rather than editing them individually.

What to expect after fixing zero-impression products

After making feed changes, allow 24–72 hours for Merchant Center to reprocess your feed and for Google's Shopping index to update. Products that were genuinely query-mismatched (title quality issue) typically start receiving impressions within this window once the title is improved.

Products that were in a bid-too-low situation are harder to diagnose because the title might already be good — they're just losing every auction. Test by temporarily raising the bid significantly (2–3x your category average) for a 7-day period. If impressions appear at the higher bid, you know the issue is competitive bid level rather than feed quality.
