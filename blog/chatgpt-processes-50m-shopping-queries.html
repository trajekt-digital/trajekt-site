ChatGPT processes 50 million shopping queries a day — is your brand visible?

OpenAI's ChatGPT has become one of the largest shopping discovery platforms in the world. UK brands without a properly configured product feed are completely invisible to all 50 million daily queries.

By Trajekt · 28 May 2026 · June 25, 2026  ·  5 min read

In the time it takes you to read this article, ChatGPT will have processed approximately 35,000 shopping queries. By the time you finish your working day, that number will be close to 3 million. In a single day, ChatGPT processes over 50 million shopping queries — and that figure is growing.

What a ChatGPT shopping query looks like

ChatGPT shopping queries aren't search queries in the Google sense. They're conversations. They include context, constraints, and often personal circumstances that would never appear in a traditional search box.

Real examples of the kinds of queries ChatGPT handles daily:

  • "I'm buying a present for my dad who's just retired — he loves fly fishing and golf. Budget £150. Any ideas?"
  • "I need new running shoes. I pronate slightly, run 30 miles a week, and train on roads and trails. What should I be looking at?"
  • "We're redesigning our living room in a Scandinavian style. We need a sofa and coffee table that work together. Budget around £2,000 for both."

These queries require contextual understanding. ChatGPT evaluates your product data to determine whether your products are genuinely relevant to the specific combination of needs, constraints, and context the user has described.

Why feed completeness matters more than keywords

Traditional Shopping optimisation is fundamentally keyword-based — you include terms in your titles and descriptions that match what people search for. ChatGPT works differently.

ChatGPT evaluates your product data for:

  • Factual accuracy: Does your description truthfully describe what the product is?
  • Completeness: Does your data include the attributes needed to evaluate fit for the query?
  • Specificity: Are the details specific enough to match to the user's precise requirements?
  • Consistency: Does your feed data match your website data?

A product with a vague, marketing-heavy description ("High quality shoes for all occasions!") will not surface for a specific query about pronation and trail running — even if it's the perfect shoe. A product with a factual, complete description covering foot support type, terrain suitability, and construction details will.

UK access: preparing now

ChatGPT Shopping is currently in US-first rollout, with UK expansion confirmed throughout 2026. For UK brands, this creates a specific strategic opportunity.

The merchants who build clean, complete, correctly-formatted ChatGPT feeds now will have a head start when UK access opens. The approval process, feed validation, and initial optimisation all take time — starting six months early versus starting the day UK access opens is the difference between a smooth launch and a scrambled one.

Register at chatgpt.com/merchants now to join the waitlist and receive early access when UK availability expands.

The invisible brand problem

From ChatGPT's perspective, a brand without a product feed simply doesn't exist. There is no fallback to website crawling for shopping recommendations — the ChatGPT shopping experience is 100% feed-driven.

This is different from SEO, where a well-structured website with good content can rank organically. In ChatGPT Shopping, if you don't have a feed, your products don't appear. Full stop.

UK brands with strong Google Shopping performance, Meta product catalogue, and TikTok Shop presence — but no ChatGPT feed — are invisible to the fastest-growing product discovery channel in the world.

The competitive advantage window

Early-mover advantage in new commerce channels has a consistent pattern. The merchants who invested in Google Shopping in 2012-2014 built position at a fraction of the cost of those who waited until 2018. The merchants who launched on TikTok Shop UK in 2023 had significantly lower CAC than those who followed in 2024.

ChatGPT Shopping is the current equivalent. The channel is growing at a rate no established channel can match. The specification is defined. The tools exist to build compliant feeds. The only question is whether you build your position now or wait until the rest of the market catches up.
