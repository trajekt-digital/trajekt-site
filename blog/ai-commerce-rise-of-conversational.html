AI Commerce

The rise of conversational commerce and what it means for your product feed

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode — AI assistants are changing how people discover products online. What the shift to conversational commerce means for UK merchants, and what your feed needs to do differently.

By Trajekt·3 Mar 2026·March 19, 2026  ·  8 min read

For fifteen years, product discovery worked the same way. Keyword query in Google. Keyword signals in your feed matched. Product appeared. The visitor clicked. That model hasn't disappeared — but a parallel model is growing fast: the person asks an AI assistant, the AI evaluates your product data against the intent behind the question, and your product either appears or it doesn't. There are no ads to bid your way in.

The three AI commerce surfaces UK merchants need to know

ChatGPT Shopping processes 50 million product-related queries daily. UK merchants need a correctly-formatted JSONL feed delivered via SFTP. ChatGPT evaluates your product data for factual richness and relevance to conversational queries.

Perplexity accepts Google Shopping XML feeds through its merchant programme — the same feed you already use for Google Merchant Center can power Perplexity commerce with minimal additional work.

Google AI Mode (AI Overviews) uses your existing Google Merchant Center feed to power AI-generated product recommendations. Feed quality matters more here than in standard Shopping, because Google's AI is evaluating semantic relevance rather than keyword matching.

What your feed needs to do differently

Traditional Shopping: Title keywords match query keywords. Keyword density in descriptions matters.

AI Commerce: Factual specificity enables contextual matching. The AI understands "Gore-Tex membrane" relates to waterproofing. "Gore-Tex Pro with 28,000mm hydrostatic head" is more useful than "waterproof". Use cases in your description help AI match to situational queries.

The opportunity gap

Right now, most UK merchants have not adapted their feeds for AI commerce. Their descriptions are marketing copy. Their ChatGPT feeds either don't exist or are converted Google Shopping feeds with HTML-heavy descriptions that fail validation.

Merchants who build AI-ready product data now will have a competitive advantage in AI commerce for as long as it takes the rest of the market to catch up.

How ChatGPT actually selects products to show

OpenAI hasn't published the full algorithm, but based on what we know about the ChatGPT Shopping feed spec and how the system responds to queries, product selection appears to be heavily influenced by: description quality (factual, specific, matching the query intent), availability (in_stock products prioritised), price accuracy (matching the live website), and feed completeness (products with all required and recommended fields). The spec explicitly states that descriptions should be "informative and accurate" — ChatGPT's underlying model is evaluating descriptions against that standard, not just keyword-matching them.

This is meaningfully different from Google Shopping, where title keyword density and match score drive a significant portion of impression share. ChatGPT rewards semantic accuracy — a description that genuinely explains what the product is and who it's for will outperform one stuffed with keywords but thin on specifics.

Perplexity AI and Google AI Mode: two more surfaces

ChatGPT is the largest AI shopping surface but not the only one. Perplexity AI has its own product discovery layer — it sources product data through its publisher programme and crawls product schema from merchant websites. Google AI Mode (formerly SGE) surfaces products directly in AI-generated search responses, drawing from Google Shopping feeds via Merchant Center. The common thread: your product data quality determines whether you appear in all three, not just one.

The practical implication is that investment in feed quality has compounding returns. A description optimised for ChatGPT Shopping (factual, specific, well-structured) also performs better in Perplexity's product discovery layer. Accurate pricing and availability in your Google Shopping feed flows into Google AI Mode. There is no separate feed to maintain for AI commerce — the same structured data, well-maintained, covers all three surfaces.

What to do right now before UK access widens

ChatGPT Shopping is US-first, with UK access expanding through 2026. The merchants who establish a presence in ChatGPT's product index early will have a data advantage — their products will accumulate shopping context before the market becomes competitive.
