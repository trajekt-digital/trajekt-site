FAQ

Frequently asked questions

eBay sellers create listings via the eBay Selling Manager, the eBay API, or third-party tools. Each listing requires: title (max 80 chars), category, condition, description, price, shipping details, and return policy. Listings can be Fixed Price or Auction format. Most catalogue-based sellers use Fixed Price listings synced from their main store.
eBay item specifics are structured product attributes (Brand, Colour, Size, Material, Style) that help buyers filter search results. They vary by category and some are required for listing approval. Including all relevant item specifics significantly improves your listing's visibility in eBay search. Trajekt maps your product attributes to eBay's item specifics automatically.
Trajekt pulls inventory from Shopify daily and generates an eBay inventory update file. When a product sells out on Shopify, Trajekt updates the eBay quantity to 0 preventing overselling. When stock is replenished, the eBay listing quantity is updated automatically.
eBay's Product Catalogue (similar to Amazon's ASIN system) has pre-existing product records for millions of products identified by EAN/barcode. Listing against a catalogue product typically improves search visibility and allows eBay to auto-populate some item specifics. Products not in the catalogue are listed without catalogue association.
Yes. Trajekt generates your eBay listings from the same Shopify or WooCommerce data as your other channels — with eBay-specific formatting, pricing, and item specific mapping applied.
eBay charges: insertion fees (zero for first 1000 listings per month for private sellers), final value fees (typically 10-12.8% depending on category), and optional promoted listings fees. Factor these into your eBay pricing strategy.
eBay Fixed Price listings can be set to run for 3, 5, 7, 10, 30 days, or as Good Till Cancelled (GTC). GTC is standard for catalogue sellers — the listing renews automatically every 30 days while stock is available.
Trajekt can generate feeds for eBay.co.uk, eBay.de, eBay.fr, eBay.com, and other eBay sites from the same product source. International listings require appropriate currency and shipping configuration.
eBay titles have an 80-character limit. Include: brand, product name, key attributes (colour, size, model number), and condition for used items. Avoid promotional language ("Amazing", "Best"). Include the most common search terms buyers use. Unlike Google Shopping, eBay titles don't need to follow a strict attribute order.
eBay requires an explicit returns policy on each listing. Specify: whether you accept returns (30 days is eBay recommended), who pays return shipping, and the condition accepted for return. Your returns policy impacts your eBay seller performance score.
eBay Promoted Listings is eBay's advertising format — you set an ad rate (percentage of sale price) and eBay boosts your listing in search results. You only pay when a buyer clicks your promoted listing and purchases within 30 days. It's separate from your product feed but uses the same listing data.
