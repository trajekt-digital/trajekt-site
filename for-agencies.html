How agencies use Trajekt across client accounts

One platform, one login, every client’s feeds managed from a single dashboard. Here’s the workflow most Trajekt agencies run.

1

Onboard a new client in under 10 minutes

Add the client store in Trajekt — Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, or BigCommerce. Trajekt pulls the full catalogue immediately. Clone your agency rule template (title optimisation, custom labels, category mapping) as a starting point and customise for the client’s category.

2

Configure channels per client

Different clients need different channels — one needs Google Shopping and Awin, another needs ChatGPT and TikTok. Each store has its own channel configuration. All channels are included in every plan so there’s no extra cost to enable a new one.

3

Set custom labels for bid management

Agency bread and butter. Set custom_label_0 by margin tier, custom_label_1 by seasonality, custom_label_2 by stock velocity — all via rules that run automatically. Your Google Ads team gets clean product groups for bid segmentation from day one.

4

Set-and-forget delivery

Each client’s feeds refresh daily or twice daily automatically. New products, price changes, stock updates, discontinued items — all handled. You get error alerts if anything fails. No daily manual tasks, no checking whether feeds ran.

5

Prove value in client reports

Trajekt’s feed quality improvements are quantifiable — before/after product count, disapproval rate, title structure changes. Screenshot the Merchant Center Diagnostics tab after the first full feed run and put it in your onboarding report.

Frequently asked questions

Trajekt for Agencies is a multi-store tier of Trajekt designed for PPC agencies managing product feed for multiple clients. It provides a single platform with separate workspaces per client store, team access controls, shareable rule templates, and automated delivery — eliminating the manual work of managing feeds across multiple clients.
Each client store is a separate workspace in Trajekt with its own source connection (Shopify, WooCommerce, XML URL), feeds, rules, and schedule. You switch between stores from a sidebar. Rules, settings, and feed configurations are isolated between stores — a rule change for one client doesn't affect others.

Trajekt is billed per agency account, not per client store. Your plan determines how many stores and products you can manage. This lets you manage multiple clients under a single subscription without per-client tool costs.
