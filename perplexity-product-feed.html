FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine that answers questions with cited sources. Its shopping functionality surfaces product recommendations in response to shopping queries. When a user asks "best running shoes under £150", Perplexity can show product recommendations with prices, ratings, and direct links. This represents a new form of AI commerce discovery.
Perplexity's merchant programme accepts Google Shopping format feeds — specifically the RSS XML format used by Google Merchant Center. This is a significant advantage: if you're already running Google Shopping via Trajekt, your existing feed URL may be compatible with Perplexity's merchant programme with little additional work.
ChatGPT Shopping uses OpenAI's proprietary JSONL format delivered via SFTP. Perplexity accepts Google Shopping XML format. This makes adding Perplexity much easier for merchants already on Google Shopping — one additional channel from your existing feed. ChatGPT requires a separate feed specification and SFTP setup.
Visit perplexity.ai/merchant or contact Perplexity's commerce team. The programme is expanding — early adoption is valuable for first-mover advantage as Perplexity's user base grows. Provide your Google Shopping compatible feed URL as part of the application.
Perplexity's AI evaluates: complete product titles with key attributes, detailed factual descriptions (no marketing fluff), accurate pricing, GTINs/barcodes, correct google_product_category assignment, and high-quality images. Products with complete structured data are better matched to natural language queries.
AI search engines like Perplexity evaluate multiple signals: feed data quality (completeness and accuracy), website authority, product reviews and ratings, price competitiveness, availability, and semantic relevance to the query. Unlike traditional keyword matching, AI engines understand product context and user intent — making factual, descriptive product data more important than keyword stuffing.
Perplexity is growing fast — from 15 million to 100+ million users within a year. As AI search adoption grows, its commerce functionality will become increasingly important. Early adoption while the programme is new provides first-mover advantage, similar to early Google Shopping adoption in 2012.
Products with clear specifications, objective comparison points, and price sensitivity perform well: electronics, appliances, books, sports equipment, and tools. Products requiring subjective evaluation (art, luxury fashion) or personalised recommendation perform less well without detailed product data.
Perplexity displays prices from your feed and cross-references them with publicly available pricing data. Price accuracy is important — significantly inflated or out-of-date prices reduce listing quality scores. Daily feed updates keep Perplexity listings current.

Rewriting descriptions to be structured, factual, and feature-rich (without marketing language) directly improves performance in AI search engines like Perplexity.
