Google Product Category — mapping guide for UK merchants

The google_product_category field is optional in Google’s spec but essential in practice. Correct, specific category mapping improves Shopping impression share, unlocks category-specific attributes, and helps Google match your products to the right buyer queries.

Why category mapping matters more than most merchants realise

Two products with identical titles, descriptions, and prices can have very different Shopping performance based solely on their GPC assignment. Here’s why:

1. Query matching signal

A product assigned to “Apparel & Accessories” (level 1) competes for all apparel queries. A product assigned to “Apparel & Accessories > Clothing > Outerwear > Coats & Jackets” (level 4) receives a stronger relevance signal for jacket-specific queries. More specific = better match quality.

2. Unlocks category-specific attributes

Certain feed attributes only activate for products in the correct GPC category. Apparel products need gender, age_group, size, and color to qualify for apparel Shopping filters. Without correct GPC, these attributes are ignored even if present in your feed.

3. Shopping campaign segmentation

Performance Max and Standard Shopping campaigns use GPC to group products. Clean category assignment makes bid-by-category segmentation straightforward. Incorrect categories make it impossible to build sensible product group structures.

How to find the right category

Three methods, in order of reliability:

  1. Download Google’s taxonomy file — available at support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324436. Open in Excel or Google Sheets, use Find (Ctrl+F) to search for keywords from your product type. The left column is the text path, the right column is the numeric ID you’ll use in your feed.
  2. Merchant Center auto-suggest — when editing a product in Merchant Center Next, the google_product_category field auto-suggests categories as you type. Start with your product type keyword and work down to the most specific match.
  3. Check Google’s automatic assignment — in Merchant Center Next, Google shows what category it automatically assigned each product. This tells you what Google thinks your product is — and whether its interpretation matches your own.

The google_product_category (GPC) attribute is a field in your Google Shopping feed that classifies your product according to Google's official product taxonomy — a hierarchical list of over 6,000 product categories. You can submit the category as either a numeric ID (e.g. 1604) or the full text path (e.g. Apparel & Accessories > Clothing > Outerwear > Coats & Jackets). Google strongly recommends using numeric IDs for stability, as text paths can change when Google updates its taxonomy.
