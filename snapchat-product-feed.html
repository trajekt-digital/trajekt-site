FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Snapchat Dynamic Product Ads (DPA) automatically generate personalised ads using your product catalogue. When a Snapchat user has interacted with products on your website (tracked via Snap Pixel), Snapchat automatically serves them ads featuring those specific products. Dynamic Product Ads are highly relevant and typically outperform static ads for ecommerce.
Snapchat accepts product catalogues in CSV or XML format — similar to Meta's catalogue spec. Required fields include id, title, description, availability, condition, price, link, and image_link. The format is close enough to Meta's that a well-structured Meta catalogue often works for Snapchat with minor adjustments.
Required fields: id, title, description, availability, condition, price (with currency code), link, and image_link. Additional recommended fields include brand, google_product_category, sale_price, item_group_id, color, and size. GTINs improve matching quality.
Snapchat reaches 21 million UK users monthly. The demographic is heavily skewed young: 75% aged 13-34. This makes Snapchat particularly valuable for fashion, beauty, entertainment, food, and lifestyle brands targeting younger UK consumers.
Snapchat Collection Ads combine a full-screen hero creative (image or video) with a row of 4 tappable product tiles from your catalogue below it. Users can tap a product tile to view details without leaving Snapchat. Collection Ads are one of the highest-converting formats for ecommerce on Snapchat.
The Snap Pixel is a JavaScript tag placed on your website. It tracks events including VIEW_CONTENT (product page views), ADD_CART (add to cart), and PURCHASE (completed orders). Each event should include the product item_id matching your catalogue. This data enables Snapchat to serve Dynamic Product Ads to users who viewed specific products.
Daily updates are recommended. Snapchat processes catalogue updates each day. Keeping availability and pricing current prevents Dynamic Ads from serving out-of-stock products. Trajekt generates and updates your Snapchat catalogue daily.
Yes. Snapchat offers AR Try-On experiences for fashion (glasses, clothing) and beauty (makeup) brands. These use 3D product models from your catalogue rather than standard flat images. AR shopping is available to brands with Snapchat Lens Studio access.

Fashion, beauty, food, and lifestyle brands see strong results. Less suitable for B2B or older demographic products.
Snapchat is a full-screen vertical (9:16) platform. For Dynamic Product Ads, standard square (1:1) product images work but vertical images fill the screen better. For Collection Ads and Story Ads, 9:16 (1080x1920px) creative is optimal. High-quality, clean product images without text overlays perform best.
