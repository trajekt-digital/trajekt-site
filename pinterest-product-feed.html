Pinterest Product Feed — what Trajekt does for you

Vertical image selection

Pinterest favours 2:3 (1000x1500px) vertical images. Trajekt rules can select the correct image orientation from your product image arrays.

📂

Google taxonomy mapping

Pinterest uses Google's Product Taxonomy. Trajekt maps your product types to the correct taxonomy IDs for better Shopping tab discovery.

🔗

UTM tracking

Trajekt adds Pinterest UTM parameters to your product URLs for accurate traffic attribution in your analytics.

Sale prices

Daily fetch URL

Pinterest fetches your Trajekt feed URL daily — keeping Product Pins current without manual uploads.

Frequently asked questions

A Pinterest Catalogue is a product data feed enabling your products to appear as shoppable Product Pins across Pinterest — search results, home feed, related Pins, and the Shopping tab. Each product becomes a Product Pin with real-time pricing, availability, and a link to your product page.
Pinterest accepts CSV, TSV, and XML. TSV is most common. Files must be UTF-8 encoded. Pinterest Business Hub also supports Google Sheets for smaller catalogues.
Required: id, title, description, link, image_link, price, and availability. Strongly recommended: google_product_category, item_group_id, additional_image_link, sale_price, brand, color, size, and condition.
Images are critical on Pinterest. Recommended ratio: 2:3 (1000x1500px). Minimum: 100x100px (too small for performance). Use clean product photography. Vertical images take more screen space in the feed.
Pinterest uses Google's Product Taxonomy for categorisation — the same as Google Shopping. Accurate categorisation improves discovery in Pinterest's Shopping tab and category browsing. Trajekt includes rules to map your product types to correct Google taxonomy IDs.
Each colour and size variant must be a separate row with unique id but shared item_group_id. Pinterest groups variants into a single Product Pin showing options. Include color and size for each variant row.
Yes. Pinterest Shopping and Catalogue functionality is fully available for UK merchants. You need a verified Pinterest Business Account with a claimed website. Shopping Ads are available in the UK.
Pinterest processes catalogue updates daily. Provide a hosted feed URL for automatic fetching. Trajekt generates your Pinterest CSV daily and provides a stable fetch URL for Pinterest Business Hub.
checkout_link is a separate URL for tracked checkout. It differentiates Pinterest-referred purchases in analytics. Trajekt can add UTM parameters to product URLs for Pinterest traffic attribution.
You need a Pinterest business account and verified website. You don't need a native Pinterest Shop. The catalogue feeds directly into Shopping Ads campaigns.
High-quality product images, clean backgrounds, no promotional overlays. Vertical 2:3 (1000x1500px) for best performance. Include multiple angles via additional_image_link.
