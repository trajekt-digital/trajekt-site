Frequently asked questions

Product feed management software connects your ecommerce store to advertising channels and marketplaces by creating, transforming, and delivering the structured product data files each platform requires. It handles format conversion (XML for Google, CSV for Meta, JSONL for ChatGPT), field mapping and optimisation, scheduled delivery, and error monitoring — replacing manual daily exports and uploads.
Shopping feed apps (like Shopify's built-in Google channel) handle one or two channels with minimal configuration. Product feed management software handles 10+ channels simultaneously, has a rules engine for data transformation, supports multiple store connections, and provides monitoring and error alerting. For merchants on more than two channels or with more than a few hundred products, software is the right category.
Key criteria for UK merchants: GBP pricing (not USD), UK-timezone support, EAN-13 validation, UK GDPR compliance for vehicle data, and support for UK-specific channels (Awin, OnBuy, Kelkoo, Idealo). Trajekt is built in the UK for UK merchants and handles all of these natively. Most US-based alternatives (Feedonomics, DataFeedWatch) require workarounds for UK-specific requirements.
Most legacy product feed management tools don't support ChatGPT Shopping — the JSONL format and SFTP delivery model are fundamentally different from Google Shopping XML. Trajekt is purpose-built for ChatGPT's feed spec, handling variant_dict calculation, HTML stripping, and automated SFTP delivery. With 50M daily ChatGPT shopping queries and UK rollout in progress, this is the most important channel most feed tools are missing.
Pricing ranges significantly by vendor. Enterprise platforms (Feedonomics, ChannelAdvisor) don't publish pricing but typically start at several hundred dollars per month with annual contracts. Mid-market tools (DataFeedWatch, Channable) start at $50-100/month. Trajekt starts at £0 (free plan) with paid plans from £19/month, making it the most accessible option for UK merchants and agencies.
The main platforms supported by most feed management tools are Shopify and WooCommerce. Trajekt also supports Magento 2/Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, PrestaShop, and any XML or CSV source feed URL. Trajekt's Shopify connection uses the Admin GraphQL API for deeper data access (metafields, collections) than most tools provide.
Modern product feed management software is designed for ecommerce managers and marketers, not developers. Trajekt's rules engine uses a visual interface — no code required at any stage. Store connections use standard API credentials generated in a few clicks. Feed delivery to platforms is fully automated once configured.
