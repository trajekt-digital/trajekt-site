BigCommerce Product Feed — what Trajekt does for you

🔌

API connection

Trajekt connects via BigCommerce's V3 Products API. Create an API account in BigCommerce admin with read-only product permissions and paste the credentials into Trajekt.

🎨

Variant handling

BigCommerce variant products with option combinations are correctly mapped — each variant appears as a separate feed row with shared item_group_id for Google and Meta grouping.

📦

Custom fields

BigCommerce product custom fields are available in Trajekt's rules engine — map brand, GTIN, material, and any other custom field to feed attributes.

💰

Sale price support

BigCommerce sale prices are pulled and mapped to sale_price in your feeds. Sale price effective dates are included for platforms that support scheduled promotions.

🖼️

Multiple images

All BigCommerce product images are pulled. Primary image maps to image_link; additional images map to additional_image_link for Google, Meta, and ChatGPT feeds.

🔄

Scheduled updates

Trajekt syncs with BigCommerce on your chosen schedule and regenerates feeds automatically.

Common BigCommerce feed problems — and how Trajekt fixes them

Price mismatches — Sale prices changing in BigCommerce but not reflected in your feed until the next crawl. Trajekt’s daily refresh (or twice-daily on Growth+) keeps prices current. A manual re-run can be triggered any time before a sale launches.
Missing GTINs — BigCommerce doesn’t have a native barcode field in all versions. Trajekt maps your custom barcode/EAN attribute directly to the gtin feed field via a rule. Branded products get their GTINs; own-brand products get identifier_exists: no.
Thumbnail images — Some BigCommerce configurations export thumbnail image URLs rather than full-size images. Trajekt maps to the full-size image URL via the API, ensuring images meet Google’s 800×800px recommended minimum.
Unoptimised titles — Default BigCommerce product names are written for on-site browsing, not Shopping search matching. Trajekt rules restructure titles to the Shopping-optimal format (Brand + Product + Attributes) without changing anything in your BigCommerce store.
No custom labels — BigCommerce has no native custom label concept for Shopping. Trajekt sets custom_label_0–4 automatically via rules — by price range, category, stock level, or any field in your BigCommerce data.
