When DataFeedWatch is the right choice

DataFeedWatch is a mature, well-documented platform that many UK agencies use for Google Shopping and Meta feed management. Its rule logic for field transformation is robust, its channel template library is broad, and it integrates with most major ecommerce platforms. For merchants who specifically need an obscure marketplace channel that Trajekt doesn't yet support, DataFeedWatch may have a template for it.

When to choose DataFeedWatch: You need a very specific marketplace template from their long-tail library, or you're deeply embedded in DataFeedWatch's existing rule structure and switching cost is high.
When to choose Trajekt: You need ChatGPT Shopping JSONL (as of July 2026, not documented in DataFeedWatch), AI description enrichment, a permanent free plan, or GBP pricing without USD conversion complexity.
