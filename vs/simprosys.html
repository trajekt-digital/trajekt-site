When Simprosys is the right choice

If you only need Google Shopping, Meta, Microsoft, and Pinterest from a Shopify store — and you’re comfortable in the Shopify app ecosystem — Simprosys is well-built, well-supported, and cheaper. It’s a solid tool for what it does.

When to choose Trajekt instead
  • ‣ You need ChatGPT Shopping as UK access rolls out in 2026
  • ‣ You want TikTok Shop, Awin, Amazon, eBay, or OnBuy feeds

  • ‣ You want AI-powered description enrichment at scale
  • ‣ You run multiple stores or manage feeds for multiple clients
