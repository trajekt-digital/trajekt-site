Frequently asked questions

Feedoptimise is a product feed management platform that supports Google Shopping, Facebook, Criteo, and other channels. It offers a Shopify app for feed export and a web dashboard for feed rule management. Pricing starts from approximately €27/month on their Lite plan (retainer plus per-feed pricing), scaling by item count and feed volume.
The main differences: Trajekt supports ChatGPT Shopping; Trajekt is priced in GBP with a permanent free plan (Feedoptimise prices in EUR from €27/month with per-feed charges); Trajekt has AI description enrichment with Claude; and Trajekt's Shopify connection uses the Admin GraphQL API for deeper data access including metafields.
Trajekt's Growth plan at £49/month covers up to 25,000 products and 3 stores with all channels included. Feedoptimise's per-feed pricing model means costs can add up as you add channels. For UK merchants, Trajekt's GBP pricing also avoids currency conversion uncertainty.
As of July 2026, we could not find ChatGPT Shopping (JSONL/SFTP) listed in Feedoptimise's supported channel documentation. Trajekt is purpose-built for the ChatGPT JSONL spec with correct variant_dict handling, clean description output, and automated SFTP delivery.
