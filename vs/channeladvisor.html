It's an enterprise product with pricing to match, typically used by large retailers with complex multichannel operations.
ChannelAdvisor has UK operations but is US-centric in its pricing model, support structure, and marketplace focus. UK merchants often find it over-engineered for feed management needs (it's primarily a marketplace listing tool) and expensive relative to feed-only alternatives. Trajekt is built in the UK, priced in GBP, and focused purely on feed management and optimisation.
