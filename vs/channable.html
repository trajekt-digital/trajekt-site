When Channable is the right choice

Channable genuinely excels at two things: very broad channel coverage (they list 2,500+ channel templates) and PPC automation. If you need to manage Amazon Sponsored Products campaigns, create and auto-update Google Ads campaigns from your feed, or distribute to niche marketplaces that smaller tools don't template, Channable's feature depth is real.

When to choose Channable: You need automated PPC campaign creation from feed data, or you need to distribute to a long tail of niche marketplaces that only a 2,500-template library covers.
When to choose Trajekt: You need ChatGPT Shopping JSONL, AI description enrichment, UK-built GBP pricing, a permanent free plan, or multi-store agency management — none of which Channable currently offers.
