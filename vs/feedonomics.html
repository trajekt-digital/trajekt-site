Frequently asked questions

Feedonomics is a US-based full-service product feed management platform, now owned by BigCommerce. It positions itself as enterprise-grade, providing both the software platform and a team of feed specialists who manage feeds on your behalf. It's well-regarded by large US retailers and agencies but comes with enterprise pricing and a US-first service model.
Yes — significantly. Feedonomics does not publish pricing, but enterprise plans typically start at several hundred dollars per month with annual contracts. Trajekt starts at £0 (free plan), £19/month (Starter), £49/month (Growth), and £99/month (Pro). UK merchants managing up to 75,000 products pay £99/month on Trajekt versus an enterprise contract on Feedonomics.
As of July 2026, Feedonomics has not publicly documented ChatGPT Shopping JSONL/SFTP as a supported output format. Trajekt generates OpenAI-compliant JSONL with correct variant_dict handling and SFTP push delivery — purpose-built for the ChatGPT Shopping format that most legacy feed tools don't support.
Feedonomics handles very large catalogues and has a managed service team that can be valuable for enterprise brands without in-house feed expertise. Trajekt's Pro and Agency plans handle up to 75,000 products and unlimited products respectively, with a self-service model. For UK merchants who prefer hands-on control over their feed configuration, Trajekt's rules engine gives more direct control than a managed service.
Feedonomics is primarily US-focused — its team and service model are built around North American ecommerce. UK merchants using Feedonomics report timezone support challenges and pricing in USD. Trajekt is built in the UK, priced in GBP, and has UK-specific handling (EAN-13 validation, GBP pricing, UK GDPR VIN exemption for vehicle feeds).

Trajekt is a self-service platform — you configure your own rules and feeds via the dashboard. There's no managed service team. If you specifically need a managed service, Feedonomics is genuinely the better fit.
