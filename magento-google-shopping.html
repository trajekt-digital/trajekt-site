Connect Magento to Google Shopping in 5 steps

1

Generate Magento API credentials

In Magento Admin: System → Integrations → Add Integration. Set Resource Access to Custom and select Catalog → Products (read) and Catalog → Categories (read). Activate the integration and copy the four OAuth credentials — Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, Access Token, and Access Token Secret.

2

Connect your store in Trajekt

In Trajekt: Add Store → Magento. Enter your store URL and API credentials. Trajekt pulls your full catalogue including all products, variants, custom attributes, and images via the Magento 2 REST API.

3

Map custom attributes and set rules

In Trajekt’s rules engine, map your GTIN/barcode attribute to gtin, your brand to brand, and configure title rules to prepend the brand and append variant attributes. Assign custom labels by price range or category.

4

Copy your Trajekt feed URL

Trajekt generates a stable feed URL for your Google Shopping output. This URL always returns your latest product data. Copy it from your Trajekt dashboard.

5

Add as Scheduled Fetch in Merchant Center

In Google Merchant Center Next: Products → Data sources → Add data source → Scheduled fetch. Paste your Trajekt feed URL and set to daily. Your Magento catalogue updates in Merchant Center automatically.

Common Magento gotcha: Magento's configurable product system is the most common source of feed errors. If you export parent products rather than child simple products, each variant appears as a single row with no colour/size data — which means Google Shopping can't filter or segment by those attributes. Trajekt specifically targets child product rows from the Magento REST API to avoid this.

How Magento product variants map to Google Shopping

Magento configurable products generate child simple product rows, each with a unique id (child SKU) and a shared item_group_id (parent SKU). This ensures each size/colour combination appears as a correct feed row without duplicating parent product listings.

Feed output — variant row example
id: "magento_variant_67890"
item_group_id: "magento_parent_12345"
title: "Brand Product Name — Navy — UK 12"
color: "Navy"  size: "UK 12"
price: "49.99 GBP"  availability: "in_stock"
