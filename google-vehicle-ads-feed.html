How it works

From your vehicle inventory to Google Shopping

Trajekt pulls data from your DMS, website feed, or inventory CSV and converts it to the exact XML format Google requires for Vehicle Ads.

Step 1

Connect your inventory

Paste your vehicle inventory feed URL (XML or CSV from your DMS or website provider). Trajekt fetches and parses the data automatically.

Step 2

Map your fields

Trajekt extracts make, model, year, condition, price, mileage, colour, and images from your source data. Missing fields can be set via rules.

Step 3

Generate the XML

Your inventory is converted to Google's Vehicle Ads XML spec with the correct g: namespace attributes, store_code, and all required fields validated.

Step 4

Upload to Merchant Center

Download the XML or use Trajekt's direct upload to Google Merchant Center. Set up twice-daily updates to keep your live inventory accurate.

Frequently asked questions about Google Vehicle Ads

Google Vehicle Ads are available to UK dealerships, and Trajekt is built specifically for the UK market — mileage in miles rather than kilometres, GBP pricing, the UK GDPR VIN exemption (Trajekt uses your internal stock ID so you don't have to expose VINs), and store_code mapping to your Google Business Profile locations. Google Vehicle Ads (also called Automotive Inventory Ads) is a Google Shopping format specifically designed for vehicle retailers. Unlike standard Shopping ads, Vehicle Ads show your actual inventory — specific makes, models, years, and prices — to car shoppers on Google Search and the Shopping tab. They use a special XML feed format submitted through Google Merchant Center and require a linked Google Business Profile.
In the UK, VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) is classified as Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and is therefore optional for Google Vehicle Ads. Instead, you must submit a unique internal stock ID [id] attribute as the alternative identifier. This ID must appear on the vehicle's landing page. Trajekt handles this UK-specific requirement automatically, using your internal stock number as the primary identifier.
Google Vehicle Ads accepts a primary feed in XML or CSV (delimited text) format. The XML format uses the standard Google Shopping RSS namespace (xmlns:g="http://base.google.com/ns/1.0") with vehicle-specific attributes. The feed is uploaded to Google Merchant Center as a new data source with "Vehicle Ads" as the destination. Trajekt generates XML format by default as it handles nested attributes more reliably.
Yes. Each vehicle in your Google Vehicle Ads feed must have its own unique landing page URL that shows the specific vehicle being advertised, with a price on the page matching your feed exactly. Google crawls these pages to verify data consistency. If you use link templates, ensure they include the store_code parameter (e.g. ?store={store_code}).
The store_code must exactly match the store code in your Google Business Profile. This is how Google matches your vehicle inventory to your physical dealership location for local inventory purposes. If you have multiple dealerships, each has its own store_code. If a store_code in your feed doesn't match a Business Profile location, those vehicles will be disapproved.
Yes. Google Vehicle Ads supports all types of vehicle inventory including caravans, motorhomes, campervans, and other recreational vehicles. The format is not restricted to cars and trucks. Trajekt has been specifically tested with UK caravan and motorhome dealer feeds and handles the specific data structure used by UK recreational vehicle inventory providers.
Google recommends updating your vehicle feed at least daily, and ideally twice daily for dealers with frequently changing inventory. After submitting updates, allow 24-48 hours for changes to sync and the review process to complete. For best results, time your feed updates to capture overnight price changes and morning stock updates before peak shopping hours.
If the price in your vehicle feed does not exactly match the price displayed on your vehicle's landing page, Google will disapprove that vehicle listing. This is the most common cause of Vehicle Ad disapprovals in the UK. Ensure your feed and website prices are always in sync and consistently include or exclude VAT. Trajekt logs warnings when potential price inconsistencies are detected.
Yes. Google Vehicle Ads require a linked Google Business Profile account with verified dealership locations. The store_code in your feed must match the store codes in your Business Profile. If you don't have access to your dealership's Business Profile, Google offers an alternative Store Feed submission route — contact Google support to request access.
Unlike standard Shopping ads, Vehicle Ads do not require vehicles to be purchasable online — they can lead users to complete the purchase in person. They use vehicle-specific attributes (make, model, year, mileage, VIN, store_code) not found in standard Shopping feeds. The google_product_category is typically "920" (Vehicles). Standard disapproval policies around direct purchase don't apply to vehicle ads.
