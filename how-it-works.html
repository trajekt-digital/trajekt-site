Publish — and stay live

Pick your channels and Trajekt builds the exact format each one needs — XML for Google, JSONL for ChatGPT, CSV for Meta and TikTok — then delivers it by URL or SFTP. From then on it refreshes on a schedule, so prices, stock and new lines are always current.

  • ✓ Correct format & delivery for each channel, automatically
  • ✓ Daily refresh (more often on paid plans)
  • ✓ Alerts if a feed errors or products drop out
