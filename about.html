Built in the UK for UK merchants

Trajekt is a product feed management platform built by ecommerce practitioners who were frustrated with the tools that existed — expensive, US-centric, slow to support new channels, and always missing the one thing you needed.

We built Trajekt to be the feed management platform we wanted to use ourselves: UK-built, transparently priced in GBP, always ahead of new channels (we had ChatGPT Shopping support before any competitor), and designed for the agencies and merchants who live in this industry every day.

What Trajekt does

Trajekt connects your Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, or BigCommerce store to every major advertising channel and marketplace — Google Shopping, ChatGPT Shopping, Meta, TikTok Shop, Pinterest, Microsoft Bing, Awin, Amazon UK, eBay, OnBuy, and more — and keeps your feeds updated automatically, every day.

Our rules engine lets you optimise titles, set custom labels, map product categories, and filter your catalogue for each channel — without touching your store. Our AI enrichment layer (powered by Claude) rewrites descriptions at scale, making your products more discoverable in both traditional Shopping and AI commerce channels.

We also built the only UK-native feed tool with full support for Google Vehicle Ads and Meta Automotive Inventory Ads — used by UK car dealerships to automate their inventory advertising across both platforms from the same data source.

Built in England. Priced in GBP. UK-timezone support. EAN-13 barcode validation. UK GDPR-compliant vehicle feed handling. Not a US product adapted for the UK market.
