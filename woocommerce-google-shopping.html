How to connect WooCommerce to Google Shopping with Trajekt

Five steps from zero to a live, daily-refreshed Google Shopping feed from your WooCommerce store.

1

Create WooCommerce REST API credentials

WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API → Add Key. Set Permissions to Read. Copy the Consumer Key and Consumer Secret shown on screen — these are displayed once.

2

Connect your store in Trajekt

In Trajekt: Add Store → WooCommerce. Enter your store URL, Consumer Key, and Consumer Secret. Trajekt pulls your full catalogue immediately — products, variations, custom attributes, and images.

3

Map your custom attributes

In Trajekt's rules engine, map your WooCommerce custom attributes to feed fields: your GTIN/EAN attribute to gtin, your Brand attribute to brand. This is the step WooCommerce's native plugin skips entirely.

4

Set up title rules

Create a rule that prepends your Brand attribute and appends colour and size variation values to each product title. Your WooCommerce product names stay unchanged on-site; Shopping gets optimised titles automatically.

5

Add your feed URL to Merchant Center

Copy your Trajekt feed URL and paste it into Google Merchant Center as a Scheduled Fetch data source. Set to daily. Merchant Center pulls your latest WooCommerce data automatically — no manual uploads ever needed.
