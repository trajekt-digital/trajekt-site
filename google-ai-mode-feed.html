Google AI Mode Feed

🧠

AI Overviews integration

Google's AI Overviews pull product data from Merchant Center. Your existing Trajekt Google Shopping feed already powers AI Mode product recommendations.

📝

AI-optimised titles

AI Mode evaluates title quality differently from standard Shopping — factual, descriptive titles with key attributes outperform keyword-stuffed ones in AI results.

🏷️

Complete attributes

AI Mode weights attribute completeness heavily. GTINs, brand, colour, size, material — the more complete your feed, the more accurately AI can match products to queries.

🔢

Structured product schema

On-page Product schema markup on your product pages reinforces your Merchant Center feed data. Trajekt's AI Rules can optimise feed descriptions to match your schema markup.

💰

Price accuracy

AI Mode surfaces prices from your Merchant Center feed. Price consistency between feed, website, and schema markup is critical for AI Overview inclusion.

🔄

Daily updates

AI Mode prioritises current, accurate product data. Daily feed updates from Trajekt keep your products eligible for AI recommendation inclusion.
