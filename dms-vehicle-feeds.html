How it works

From your DMS to live vehicle ads

The path is the same regardless of which system holds your stock.

Step 1

Connect your stock

From a website feed, a DMS export, or a system like Navigator — Trajekt reads your live vehicle inventory.

Step 2

We map & clean it

Into the Google Vehicle Ads and Meta shapes: titles, price in GBP inc-VAT, mileage in miles, VIN optional (UK), store_code, sold stock flagged out.

Step 3

Rules & margin

Your own rules run, and margin data (retail vs cost) is available for bidding on the stock that actually makes you money.

Step 4

Delivered & kept current

Google Merchant Center and Meta, refreshed on a schedule so prices stay accurate and sold vehicles drop out.
