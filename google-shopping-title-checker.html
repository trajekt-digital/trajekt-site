What the checker looks for

Product titles are the single highest-impact field in a Google Shopping feed — they decide which searches you show for and how often you get clicked. This tool checks your titles against the patterns that consistently perform (and the ones that get products disapproved):

📏 Length

Aim for ~50–150 characters, with the key words in the first 70 — that’s what Google shows.

🚫 Promotional text

“Sale”, “free shipping”, “% off” and price claims aren’t allowed and risk disapproval.

🔄 Front-loading

Lead with brand and product type — not a colour or size — so the most-searched words come first.

🏷️ Attributes

Include what buyers filter by: colour, size, material, model. More matched attributes, more impressions.

These are proven heuristics, not Google’s internal ranking. Trajekt’s AI enrichment applies the same principles across thousands of products with far more nuance — and rewrites titles per channel (Google, ChatGPT, Meta) from one catalogue.
