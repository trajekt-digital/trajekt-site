What most free feed tools actually are

The word "free" in feed management usually means one of three things — none of them truly free.

⏱

Time-limited trial

14 or 30 days free, then full pricing. You invest time setting it up, then face a billing decision before you've properly evaluated it.

📴

Crippled free tier

Free plan that excludes key features — no rules engine, no scheduling, or so few products it's not representative of real usage.

📥

Freemium bait

Free for one channel, paid for everything else. Or free to connect but paid to actually deliver the feed.
