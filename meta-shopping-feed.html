FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A Meta shopping catalogue is a product data file uploaded to Meta Commerce Manager. It powers Dynamic Product Ads on Facebook and Instagram, Facebook Shop, Instagram Shop, and Advantage+ Catalogue campaigns. Required fields include id, title, description, availability, condition, price, link, image_link, and brand.
Meta accepts CSV, TSV, RSS XML, and ATOM XML. CSV is most common. Commerce Manager also supports Google Sheets for smaller catalogues. All files must be UTF-8 encoded. Trajekt generates correctly formatted CSV with all required headers.
Required: id, title, description, availability, condition, price, link, image_link, and brand. Strongly recommended: additional_image_link, sale_price, item_group_id, color, size, gender, age_group, google_product_category.
Similar required fields but different formats and delivery. Google uses XML submitted to Merchant Center. Meta uses CSV uploaded to Commerce Manager. Meta has unique fields like rich_text_description. Item grouping and variant handling also differ.
DPAs are automated ads showing relevant products from your catalogue to users who previously viewed or interacted with your products. The Meta Pixel with standard events (ViewContent, AddToCart) including product id enables automatic retargeting with the exact products someone browsed.
Meta processes updates daily with a scheduled fetch URL. For frequently-changing stock or prices, configure hourly updates. Outdated availability wastes ad spend and frustrates customers.
item_group_id groups product variants under a single parent. Without it, each variant appears unrelated. With correct grouping, Meta shows the most relevant variant — e.g. the size someone previously viewed.
Minimum 500x500px. Optimal: 1080x1080px square or 1200x628px landscape. No promotional text overlays in the primary image. Multiple images per product via additional_image_link improve ad creative selection.
