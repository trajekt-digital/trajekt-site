Frequently asked questions

Product feed management is the process of connecting your ecommerce store to advertising channels and marketplaces by creating, maintaining, and optimising the structured data files those platforms need to display your products. It covers generating the correct file format for each channel, keeping prices and stock current, applying optimisation rules, and monitoring for errors — all automatically.
Without a feed management tool, you export CSVs from your store, reformat them manually for each channel, upload them individually, and repeat this daily. For two or more channels with any catalogue size, this process is too time-consuming and error-prone to sustain. A tool automates the entire cycle: connect once, get all channels updated automatically.

A product feed (also called a data feed, product data feed, or catalogue feed) is a structured file — typically XML, CSV, or JSONL — containing your product inventory with attributes like title, price, image URL, and availability.
