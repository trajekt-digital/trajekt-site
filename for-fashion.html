Fashion feed requirements — what’s different

Fashion apparel has more required and recommended attributes than almost any other category on Google Shopping. Get these right and you unlock better match quality, more filter placements, and lower CPCs.

👒

item_group_id

Groups all variants (sizes & colours) of the same garment as a single Shopping listing with size/colour selectors. Without it, each size appears as a separate product — cluttering results and splitting performance data.

🎨

color

Each colour variant needs its own feed row with the exact colour value matching your product page. Enables Shopping colour filters. Google cross-checks feed colour vs page colour — inconsistency causes disapprovals.

👾

size

Use UK sizing notation for UK products (UK 10, UK 12). Include size_type (regular, petite, plus, maternity) and size_system: UK. These unlock Shopping size filter placements that significantly increase CTR.

👷

gender

Required for most apparel products. Values: male, female, unisex. Enables gender-based Shopping filters. Missing gender on apparel is a common UK fashion disapproval cause.

🧑

age_group

Values: adult, kids, infant, toddler, newborn. Adults is the default for most UK fashion brands, but must be specified explicitly for products in the Apparel & Accessories category.

📷

Images: apparel rules

Apparel images must show the product on a model or mannequin (Google may penalise flat-lay as primary image). White or neutral background. Minimum 250x250px, recommended 800x800px. No promotional text overlays.

Fashion title formula

The optimal Google Shopping title structure for UK fashion:

Brand + Gender + Name + Key attribute + Colour + UK Size

Apparel must also include item_group_id to correctly group all sizes and colours of the same garment as a single Shopping listing.
